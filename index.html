What's real now

  • Bye-Bye Sugar Bugs follows the GB cycle: READ the story video, QUESTION, THINK, EXPRESS, then CREATE your own book.
  • QUESTION: the sugar bug, toothpaste, apple and lollipop ask questions, answer in their own recorded voices, then the child asks Gerry "why?" questions.
  • THINK: sort snacks into Happy teeth or Sugar bugs love it, then put the brushing steps in order.
  • EXPRESS: listen, say each sentence into the microphone, and hear your own voice. Recordings stay on this device.
  • CREATE: your book maker with tracing and coloring. The finished book opens with page turns and fills the frames in My Library.
  • Explore the cover stays as a bonus button under the book.

Still to come

  • Sharing with friends needs parent accounts, so the Share button explains this for now.
  • Saved books stay on this device only, in this browser.
  • The other three books, and Levels A and C, show stand-in quests until their activities are made.
  • The activity pages keep their current design. Restyling them to match the library comes next.
