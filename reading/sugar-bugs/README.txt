# Bye-Bye Sugar Bugs - the interactive picture (GP, Oct 2026)

WHAT IT IS
One picture, our Bye-Bye Sugar Bugs cover, is the whole lesson. Every object in it is a button for a 3-to-4-year-old:
tap it and the picture zooms into that object. One big button zooms back out. Nothing to read first, no menus, no sign-in.

THE LESSONS INSIDE THE PICTURE
- The title: a short clip of our film (after a child has done three lessons).
- The teeth, the sugar cubes, the toothpaste, the lollipop, the sugar bug and the electric toothbrush TALK: each says
  a few short lines about itself (English or Korean), the words light up as they are spoken, and a small act goes with
  most of them (brush the teeth, drag a sugar cube, squeeze the tube from the bottom, find the sugar bug, switch the brush on).
- The donut, the apple and the soda can are WORDS: the word is said and shown with one sentence; tap it to hear it again.

THE FUN LINES (every line gives the child something to do, say or try tonight)
- Teeth: "We are your teeth! Brush us in little circles, top and bottom, front and all the way back. Sing a whole song
  while you brush, and the sugar bugs run away!"
- Sugar: "I am sugar. I hide in cookies, juice, and candy. After something sweet, drink some water and swish it around.
  Swish, swish, swish!"
- Toothpaste: "Squeeze me from the bottom, never from the middle! Just a little dab, as big as a pea. Then brush, and I
  make the sugar bugs run!"
- Sugar bug: "I am a sugar bug! I hide where you forget to brush, behind your back teeth. Brush back there, and I have
  to run away!"
- Lollipop: "I am a lollipop, full of sugar. Save me for after a meal, then drink a big glass of water. Water washes the
  sugar away before the bugs can find it!"
- Electric toothbrush: "I am an electric toothbrush. I buzz and I spin! Hold me still on each tooth and count to three.
  One, two, three, next tooth!"
- Apple: "Apple. Crunch, crunch! An apple scrubs your teeth while you chew it."
- Soda: "Soda. One can of soda holds ten spoonfuls of sugar. Pick water, and the bugs stay hungry!"
- Donut: "Donut. A donut is sweet and sticky. Sticky food hugs your teeth, so rinse with water after a donut."
  The apple and the soda also let the child choose which snack is good for the teeth.
- The toothbrush is the WRITING lesson: the child traces the word "toothbrush" with a finger, one letter at a time,
  and the traced word is kept.
- The little library in the corner shows the child's traced words, kept under the child's name on that device.
There is no colouring and no drawing in this page.

HOW TO PUT IT ON A WEBSITE
1. Upload this whole folder as it is (index.html + the assets folder) to any web space - a plain static host is enough.
2. Link to the folder (or to index.html). It works at any address and in any modern phone or desktop browser.
3. Keep the folder structure: index.html must sit next to the assets folder.
It also opens by double-clicking index.html on a computer.

NOTES
- Sound: on iPhone/iPad tap once anywhere first (browsers need a touch before they play sound).
- Language: the KOR / ENG switch is at the top of the first screen and is remembered.
- Everything in the picture, the sounds and the voices is GP's own work.
